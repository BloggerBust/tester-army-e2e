The built-in agent's rules (`BASE_RULES`) now tell the model not to press a save or submit control again when the first press shows no change or is still in progress. The model must first check the screen for a confirmation, an error, a new location, or the changed record. It presses again only when that check shows the first press did not commit, or when the tap only closed the on-screen keyboard, and does not press again when it cannot tell. A step that asks for several commits still presses once for each. It also stays on the step's screen instead of navigating away to confirm a save, so a save that shows no confirmation on that screen can end the step `failed`; name the check in the step ("save the product, then confirm it appears in the product list"). A first press that reached nothing and left no trace, such as a click before the page was ready, is not pressed again either, since it looks the same as a save with no feedback; wait for the page before the save. The loop guard gives the built-in looks more room while a save is still in progress: `observe` and plain `screenshot`, in any order, now warn at six looks with no change and force a verdict at ten, where every other tool keeps three and five; a look whose listing, location, or tabs changed starts the count again, a change in pixels alone does not, and a wait that keeps changing is warned at twelve looks and ended at twenty. Before, the same look repeated stopped at five; such a wait, in a step or in an exploring `agent.assert` with a raised budget, can now spend up to five more model calls on an unchanged screen and up to fifteen more on one that keeps changing. A wait that mixes `observe` and `screenshot` fell under the cycle limits before and now has the same limits of ten and twenty looks. The step budget and deadline still bound the whole step.
