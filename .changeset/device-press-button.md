`device.pressButton(button)` presses the `'volume-up'`, `'volume-down'`, or `'power'` button as a recorded `device.pressButton` step. An Android emulator or phone takes all three, and a physical iPhone or iPad the volume buttons; an iOS simulator is `UNSUPPORTED_CAPABILITY`. [device.pressButton](https://e2e.com/docs/reference/mobile#pressbutton)
